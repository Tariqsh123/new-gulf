    The UAE Paperwork, Explained

    General self-publishing guides skip the local steps. We do not.

    ISBN

    In the UAE, an ISBN is a legal requirement for published books. The National Library and Archives issues them free through its online ISBN system. We guide you through the application and check the number appears correctly on your copyright page and back cover.

    Legal deposit

    After publication, three copies of your book are deposited with the National Library and Archives. We remind you and explain how.

    Content rules

    Books circulated in the UAE market fall under UAE Media Council rules, including age classification. We tell you what may apply to your title before you print.

    Amazon’s tax interview

    Amazon asks authors outside the US to complete a tax interview before paying royalties. We walk you through each question, and you submit it yourself in your own account. For advice on your own tax position, speak to an accountant.
