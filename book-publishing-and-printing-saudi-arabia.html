    Before You Print: The Saudi Steps, in Order

    In Saudi Arabia the order matters, because some numbers must exist before your book goes to the printer.

    1. Permit. Printed books need the relevant authority's permit before printing. [Confirm the current permit authority and steps]
    2. Deposit number. King Fahd National Library issues a deposit number before publication, and it must appear in your book. Under the library's rules, the number lapses after six months unless the work is printed and two copies are deposited. [Confirm current rules]
    3. ISBN. The library also acts as the national ISBN centre, so we apply for your ISBN with you as the publisher.
    4. Print, then deposit. Only then do files go to print, and the copies go to the library.

    We prepare each step, tell you what is needed and when, and place the numbers correctly on your copyright page and back cover.
