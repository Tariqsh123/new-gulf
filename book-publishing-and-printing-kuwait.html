    Kuwait-Ready Guidance

    Publishing and printing in Kuwait comes with local requirements. We walk you through ISBN and legal deposit with the National Library of Kuwait, and explain the Ministry of Information steps that may apply under Kuwait’s Publications Law before your book is printed or circulated. This is practical guidance, not legal advice, and we confirm the current rules with you before print.

    ISBN

    The National Library of Kuwait handles ISBNs. We guide you through the application and check the number appears correctly on your copyright page and back cover. [Confirm whether “registered with you as the publisher” holds for Kuwait]

    Legal deposit

    Kuwait has legal deposit requirements, and the National Library of Kuwait is involved. We tell you what applies to your book and guide you through it.

    Publications Law

    Kuwait’s Publications Law (Law No. 3 of 2006) includes printer notification to the Ministry of Information and deposit of copies. The law was amended in 2020 to end prior approval of imported books. We explain the steps that may apply to your book before print. [Check with a Kuwaiti lawyer before stating specifics]

    Amazon’s tax interview

    Amazon asks authors outside the US to complete a tax interview before paying royalties. We walk you through each question, and you submit it yourself in your own account. For advice on your own tax position, speak to an accountant.
