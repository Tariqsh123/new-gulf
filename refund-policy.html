    Refund Policy

    This Refund Policy explains when and how you can request a refund for services bought from Gulf Books Publisher. It should be read together with our Terms and Conditions and the written quote or agreement for your project. If anything is unclear, ask us before you pay.


    Ask Us a Question Chat on WhatsApp

    1. Who We Are

    Gulf Books Publisher (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a self-publishing services company operated by Gulf Books Publisher, with its registered address at Sharjah Publishing City, Gulf Books Publisher, Sharjah, UAE.. In this policy, “you” means the author, company or organisation that buys our services.

    2. Scope of This Policy

    This policy applies to all paid services we provide, including book publishing and Amazon KDP set-up, editing and proofreading, cover design and formatting, ghostwriting and book writing, printing, audiobook production, children’s book publishing, corporate and institutional books, and marketing and author websites. It does not apply to third-party costs such as printing, shipping, advertising spend, stock images, fonts or platform fees, which are handled under section 9.

    3. Our 100% Refund Promise

    Our packages are covered by our 100% refund policy. If we fail to deliver the service described in your written quote, or if the work you receive is materially different from what was agreed, you may be entitled to a full refund of the fees you paid us for that service. To claim a refund, write to Info@gulfbookspublisher.com and follow the steps in section 7.

    4. When You Are Eligible for a Refund

    • You cancel your project before any work has started on that service, in which case you receive a full refund of the fees paid for it.
    • You cancel a service after work has started, in which case we refund the portion of the fee for work not yet carried out, less any costs already committed on your behalf.
    • We cannot deliver a service you have paid for, or we stop work for reasons within our control.
    • The delivered work does not match the written quote or agreement and we are unable, after a reasonable revision round, to bring it in line.
    • The refund request is made within 14 days of delivery of the relevant service, unless a longer period is stated in your quote or required by law.

    5. When Refunds Are Not Available

    Refunds are not normally available in the following cases:

    • You have already approved and signed off a deliverable, such as a final cover, interior file or proof, and the issue is one that was visible and could have been corrected before approval.
    • The service has been fully delivered and used, such as a completed edit, a finished audiobook or a published listing.
    • You change your mind about the creative direction, genre, design style or marketing approach after work has begun.
    • The request arises from errors in the manuscript or information you supplied to us.
    • The request relates to third-party costs, such as printing, shipping, advertising spend, stock images, fonts or platform fees (see section 9).
    • The request relates to sales, rankings, reviews, awards, bestseller status or income, which we do not and cannot guarantee.
    • You have broken the Terms and Conditions, for example by providing infringing or unlawful content.

    6. Partial Refunds

    Where a project includes several services and only some of them are affected, we refund the fees paid for the affected services. Where work on a service has started but not been completed, we refund the portion of that fee for work not yet carried out. We always explain the calculation in writing before issuing a partial refund.

    7. How to Request a Refund

    To request a refund, please follow these steps:

    1. Write to Info@gulfbookspublisher.com with “Refund Request” in the subject line.
    2. Include your full name, the project or invoice number, the service concerned and the reason for your request.
    3. Attach any relevant evidence, such as correspondence, drafts or the written quote.
    4. We will acknowledge your request within 5 working days and give you a written decision within 14 working days of receiving the complete information.

    8. How We Process Refunds

    • Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method wherever possible.
    • We aim to process approved refunds within 14 working days of our written decision. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to show the credit.
    • Where the original payment method cannot be used, we will agree an alternative with you in writing.
    • Currency conversion differences, bank charges and payment gateway fees charged by third parties may be deducted where they cannot be recovered.

    9. Refunds for Third-Party Costs

    Costs we pay to third parties on your behalf, such as printing, shipping, advertising spend, stock images, licensed fonts, ISBN fees or platform fees, are shown separately in your quote. These costs are governed by the third party’s own terms and refund rules. We will help you pursue a refund from the third party where possible, but we cannot refund amounts that the third party does not return to us.

    10. Cancellations

    You may cancel a project at any time by writing to us. If you cancel, we stop work on the date we receive your written notice and calculate any refund under sections 4 and 6. If we cancel a project for reasons within our control, you receive a full refund of the fees paid for work not delivered. If we cancel because of a breach of these terms or a request to publish unlawful content, refunds are handled under the Terms and Conditions.

    11. Chargebacks and Disputes

    If you are unhappy with a payment, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. Raising a chargeback or payment dispute without contacting us may delay the process. Nothing in this policy affects your rights under the mandatory consumer protection laws of your country.

    12. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The version published on this page on the date you accept a quote applies to that project. For later projects, the updated policy applies.

    13. Contact

    Questions about this Refund Policy? Contact us at Info@gulfbookspublisher.com, by WhatsApp on +971 58 196 9313, or at Sharjah Publishing City, Gulf Books Publisher, Sharjah, UAE.. See our Contact page.
