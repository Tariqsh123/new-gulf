    The Qatar Paperwork, Explained

    Qatar has its own rules, and general guides skip them.

    ISBN and legal deposit

    ISBNs and legal deposit numbers in Qatar are handled through Qatar National Library, and Qatar's legal deposit law requires copies of published works to be given to the national library. We guide you through both and check the numbers appear correctly in your book. [Confirm current application steps and fees]

    Ministry of Culture approval

    Qatar's printing and publications law gives the Ministry of Culture oversight of printed material, and books sold through local bookstores go through its approval process. We explain what applies to your title before you print, and we can plan an eBook or print-on-demand route so your launch does not wait on it. [Confirm current Ministry process and timelines]

    Amazon's tax interview

    Amazon asks authors outside the US to complete a tax interview before paying royalties. We walk you through it. For tax advice, speak to an accountant.
