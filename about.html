    Authors in the GCC region kept telling us the same three things. Nobody would state the pricing openly mentioned on the website, Many publishers worked only in English and it was often impossible to check the team members was behind the company.     Who We Work With

    We work with first-time and established authors in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman, and with authors in other countries who want to reach Gulf readers. Our clients include novelists, memoir writers, professionals publishing a first book, children’s authors, academics, and companies and schools producing books under their own brand. Everything can be done online, by video, phone or WhatsApp, with secure manuscript upload.
