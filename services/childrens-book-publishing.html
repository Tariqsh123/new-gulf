Why Authors Choose a Dedicated Children’s Book Publisher

Children’s books fail in small, specific ways. A rhyme that stumbles when read aloud. A character whose face changes from page to page. A page count that does not fit a printer’s rules. Arabic text that breaks when the layout is mirrored. Freelance gigs and print-only services rarely catch these problems, because each person sees only one piece of the book.

We work differently. Every project has a named project manager and a small team of editor, illustrator and designer who work from the same style guide, so the story, the pictures and the layout agree with each other. Arabic and English children’s books are handled under one roof, by native speakers of each language. You see tracked changes on every edit and approve illustration sketches before colour work begins. The same team then prepares your files for print and Amazon KDP, so nothing is lost between creative work and publication.

Writing and Editing for Young Readers

A good children’s story sounds right when it is read aloud at bedtime, so we edit with the ear as well as the eye. Our children’s book writing and editing service starts with the age of your reader. Board books for toddlers, picture books for ages three to six and early readers for ages six to nine each need different sentence length, vocabulary and pacing. Picture books usually run to 32 pages, so every line has to earn its place.

We offer developmental editing for plot, character and message, line editing for rhythm and rhyme, copy editing for grammar and consistency, and a final proofread on the laid-out pages, where most late errors appear. Most authors do not need every level, and we will recommend the lowest one that works. If you have only a rough draft or an idea, we can help you shape it into a story that holds up across the page turns.

Children’s Book Illustration and Picture-Book Layout

Illustration is the largest part of most children’s books, so we plan it carefully. Together we choose a style, such as watercolour, digital painting or clean flat colour, and our illustrator produces a character sheet so your hero looks the same on every page. You approve rough sketches before any final colour work begins, which keeps changes small and quick.

Our designers then lay out each spread with the text placed for easy reading, away from faces and key action, and inside the safe margins a printer needs. For colour interiors we prepare files with the correct bleed, trim size and image resolution. If you already have your own illustrator, we are happy to work with their artwork and handle the layout, cover and print-ready files.

Arabic and English Children’s Books

Arabic children’s books need more than translation. At Gulf Books Publisher, native Arabic editors and designers work alongside our English team, so both languages get the same care. For early readers we can add full diacritics (tashkeel), which help children learn to read, and we check hamza, ta marbuta and Arabic punctuation throughout. We also advise on register: clear Modern Standard Arabic for narration and, where the story calls for it, a light touch of Gulf dialect in dialogue.

Layout matters just as much. Arabic books turn right to left, with the spine on the right, so text, page numbers and illustrations must be arranged accordingly. For bilingual children’s books, we can place Arabic and English on the same spread or produce two matching editions, and we choose clear, child-friendly fonts for each script. Arabic names inside English text are spelled consistently from the first page to the last.

Print and Digital Formats: Amazon KDP, eBook, Paperback and Hardcover

We prepare your children’s book for the format you want to sell. For Amazon KDP, that means paperback and hardcover files with the right trim size, bleed and cover template, plus a fixed-layout eBook that keeps picture-book pages intact on screen. We help you register an ISBN, set up your KDP listing and choose the categories and keywords that parents actually search for. Colour interiors cost more to print than black and white, so we show you the options before you decide. If you prefer a local print run for schools, libraries or bookshops, we prepare print-ready PDFs to your printer’s specification. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does children’s book publishing cost in the UAE and the Gulf?

The cost depends on page count, illustration, language and format, and illustration is usually the largest part. We give an itemised quote after hearing about your story, so you see each stage separately and pay only for what your book needs.

Do you publish children’s books in Arabic as well as English?

Yes. Native Arabic and English editors and designers work in one workflow. We handle diacritics, hamza, Arabic punctuation and right-to-left layout, and we also produce bilingual editions with both languages on one spread or in matching books.

Do I need to provide my own illustrations?

No. We can illustrate your book from scratch or work with artwork you already have. In both cases we handle layout, cover design and print-ready files, and you approve sketches before final artwork is completed.

How long does it take to publish a children’s book?

Many picture books take a few months from approved text to print-ready files, depending on page count and illustration style. We agree a written schedule up front and tell you plainly if a deadline looks unrealistic.

Can you publish my children’s book on Amazon KDP?

Yes. Gulf Books Publisher prepares paperback, hardcover and fixed-layout eBook files to KDP specifications, registers your ISBN and sets up your listing. You approve everything before it goes live.

Will I see every change made to my text?

Yes. Editing is done with tracked changes, and you also receive a clean copy. You approve the illustration sketches and the final layout before we prepare files for print or publication.

Do you work with authors in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman?

Yes. We work with authors across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman remotely, by video call, email and shared files, and we prepare your book for the print and delivery options available in your country.
