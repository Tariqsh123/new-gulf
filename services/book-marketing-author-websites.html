    Why Authors Choose a Planned Approach to Book Marketing

    Most authors try marketing in bursts: a few social posts at launch, a discount, then silence. Agencies that sell generic packages, guaranteed reviews or “bestseller” badges often cost a lot and leave authors with nothing they own.

    We build assets that last. You own your website, your domain, your email list and your content. Every activity ties back to one goal: more of the right readers reaching your book page and choosing to buy. A project manager explains each step in plain language, and you see what has been done and why. Because publishing, design and marketing sit under one roof, your cover, book description and website all say the same thing in the same style.

    Author Website Design: Your Home Base Online

    An author website gives readers a place you control. It answers the questions a curious reader has: who you are, what the book is about, where to buy it and how to hear from you.

    Our author websites include a clear home page, an about page, a book page with buy links, a sample chapter or extract, a contact form and an email sign-up. They load quickly, work on phones and use proper page titles and descriptions so search engines can read them. For Arabic and bilingual sites, we build right-to-left layouts, choose readable Arabic fonts and check that menus, forms and buttons behave correctly in both languages. You can update the site yourself, and we hand over everything you need to manage it, including domain and hosting details in your name.

    Arabic and English Book Marketing

    Marketing in Arabic is not translated English marketing. At Gulf Books Publisher, native Arabic and English teams write copy for each audience, choose the tone that suits it and check that dialect, spelling and cultural references fit your readers. For bilingual authors, we can run Arabic and English campaigns side by side with a consistent brand, covering your website, listing, social posts and email. We also advise which platforms your readers actually use, rather than spreading your effort across all of them.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What do book marketing services include?

    They include a plan, Amazon listing improvements, launch materials, email set-up and, where useful, paid ads. Gulf Books Publisher agrees the exact scope in writing first, so you know what is included and what is not.

    Can you guarantee book sales or bestseller status?

    No. No honest provider can. We improve how your book is presented and shown to readers, report what happens in plain language, and recommend what to change next.

    Do I need an author website?

    Most authors benefit from one. It gives you a home online that you own, a place to collect emails and a page readers can find through search, beyond what any single platform allows.

    Can you build Arabic and bilingual author websites?

    Yes. Native Arabic and English teams build right-to-left and bilingual layouts, check fonts, menus and forms in both languages, and write copy for each audience instead of translating word for word.

    Will you buy reviews for my book?

    No. Paid or fake reviews break platform rules and can harm your book. We help you ask real readers for honest reviews and focus on listing quality and launch planning.

    How much does book marketing cost in the UAE and the Gulf?

    It depends on scope, languages, campaign length and ad budget. Websites and launch plans are usually one-off projects, and ongoing work is monthly. We send an itemised quote so you see each cost.

    Do you work with authors in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman?

    Yes. We work with authors across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman remotely, by video call, shared documents and written updates, and we shape the plan around your market.
