    Who Can Become an Author With Us

    We welcome:

    • First-time authors with a finished or near-finished manuscript
    • Authors with only an idea or a rough draft, who need writing or ghostwriting support
    • Professionals and experts turning their knowledge into a book
    • Arabic-language authors and bilingual authors
    • Children’s authors who need illustration and layout
    • Companies, schools and institutions producing books under their own brand
    • Authors outside the Gulf who want to reach Gulf readers

    What We Do Not Publish

    We publish only content that is lawful and that you have the right to publish. We may decline or stop a project that includes plagiarised or copied material, content that infringes someone else’s rights, unlawful content, or material that promotes hatred or violence. If we have a concern, we will explain it to you plainly and discuss options before taking any decision.

    Your Path to a Published Book

    1. Free consultation and quoteSend your draft in any format. We talk through your goals and return an itemised quote with a written timeline. The 30-minute consultation is free, with no payment and no obligation.
    2. EditingOur editors improve clarity, structure and consistency. You receive tracked changes and a clean copy.
    3. Cover, formatting and ISBNWe design the cover, format the interior for print and eBook, and register your ISBNs with you as the publisher.
    4. Platform set-up and print proofWe set up Amazon KDP and your other retailers, write the metadata and order a printed proof so you can hold the book before release.
    5. Launch and supportYour book goes live with a launch plan, and we stay available for marketing questions, reprints and your next title.

    Our typical timeline is about 2 weeks. Longer manuscripts, heavier editing, illustrations or printed proofs can add time, so we agree the schedule in writing before work begins.

    Questions Authors Ask

    Do I need a finished manuscript?

    No. You can start with a draft, an outline or an idea. We will tell you whether your project needs editing, writing support or ghostwriting first.

    Will I really keep my rights?

    Yes. You own your rights, your ISBNs are registered with you as the publisher, and your Amazon KDP account stays in your name.

    Can I write in Arabic?

    Yes. You can publish in Arabic, English or both, with native Arabic editors and right-to-left formatting for print and eBook.

    Can I publish if I live outside the Gulf?

    Yes. Everything is handled online, and we explain which ISBN and registration route suits your country.
