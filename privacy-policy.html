    Privacy Policy

    Gulf Books Publisher (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit gulfbookspublisher.com, contact us or use our publishing services, why we collect it, how we protect it and what choices you have.

    1. Who Is Responsible for Your Data

    The controller of your personal data is Gulf Books Publisher, licence number +971 58 196 9313, of Sharjah Publishing City, Gulf Books Publisher, Sharjah, UAE.. For any privacy question, contact +971 58 196 9313.

    2. What Data We Collect

    Information you give us. When you request a quote, book a consultation, use WhatsApp, fill in a form or become a client, we may collect:

    • your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number and country
    • details about your book and project, such as language, length, services needed and timeline
    • your manuscript, drafts, images, cover ideas and other files you upload or send
    • billing details needed to take payment
    • messages and feedback you send us, and review content you agree to share

    Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we may collect your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page and approximate location, using cookies and similar technologies (see section 8).

    Information from others. If you contact us through a platform such as Trustpilot or Google, or if a colleague or company gives us your details for a corporate project, we receive the details they share.

    We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under [age] without a parent’s or guardian’s involvement.

    3. Why We Use Your Data

    We use your data to:

    • respond to enquiries and prepare quotes and consultations
    • deliver the services you buy, including editing, design, formatting, ISBN registration, platform set-up, printing and marketing
    • take payment and keep financial records
    • communicate with you about your project, including by email, phone and WhatsApp
    • improve our website and services
    • send you marketing emails, only where you have agreed or where the law allows, with an unsubscribe link in every message
    • publish reviews, only with your permission
    • meet legal, tax and accounting duties and protect against fraud and misuse

    4. Our Legal Basis

    Depending on where you live, we rely on: your consent (for example, marketing emails and non-essential cookies); the need to carry out a contract or steps you ask for before one (quotes and projects); our legitimate interests (running and improving our business and keeping it secure), balanced against your rights; and legal obligations.

    5. Your Manuscript Is Confidential

    We treat your manuscript and project files as confidential. We use them only to prepare quotes and deliver the services you choose, and only the people who work on your project can access them. We will sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) on request. We do not use your manuscript to train artificial intelligence models, and we do not publish or share it without your permission.

    6. Who We Share Data With

    We share data only where needed, with:

    • Service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, email and form tools, cloud storage, video-call tools, payment processors, analytics and customer-management software
    • Project suppliers such as editors, translators, narrators, illustrators, designers and printers, who are bound by confidentiality
    • Platforms and authorities you ask us to deal with, such as Amazon KDP, other retailers, ISBN agencies and national libraries, which receive the details needed to register or publish your book
    • Professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers
    • Authorities, where the law requires it

    We do not sell your personal data.

    We work with authors across the Gulf and beyond, and some of our providers are based outside your country. Your data may therefore be processed in other countries, including . Where the law requires, we use safeguards such as contracts and security measures to protect it.

    8. Cookies

    We use cookies and similar tools to make the site work, to understand how it is used and, with your consent where required, to support marketing. You can change your cookie settings through and through your browser.

    9. How Long We Keep Your Data

    We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above:

    • Enquiries that do not become projects:
    • Project files and records:
    • Financial and tax records:
    • Marketing contacts: until you unsubscribe

    10. How We Protect Your Data

    We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including secure file upload, access limited to those who need it, and confidentiality duties for our team and suppliers. No online system is completely secure, so please do not send us highly sensitive information, such as passwords or full payment card details, by email or WhatsApp. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authority where the law requires.

    Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have the right to:

    • ask for access to the personal data we hold about you
    • ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete
    • ask us to delete your data

    • withdraw consent at any time, such as by unsubscribing from marketing
