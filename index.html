      Frequently Asked Questions About Gulf Books Publisher

      How do I publish a book in the Gulf?

      Start with a free consultation, then editing, cover design, formatting and ISBN registration, followed by platform set-up on Amazon KDP and other retailers and a printed proof. A full-service publisher such as Gulf Books Publisher manages each step and sends a written quote and timeline first. You keep your rights throughout.

      How much does it cost to publish a book in the UAE or Saudi Arabia?

      Cost depends on word count, editing depth, cover and interior complexity, illustrations, print format and marketing. Our starting prices are AED 1,500 to publish an eBook, AED 2,000 to edit, design and publish, and AED 3,500 for full publishing and launch. Your quote itemises every service, so you only pay for what your book needs. See our cost guide for a full breakdown.

      Do I need an ISBN to publish my book?

      An ISBN is how bookstores, libraries and most online retailers identify your book, so you will need one for those channels. If you are selling only an eBook on Amazon, its own ASIN may be sufficient. We register your ISBNs under your name as publisher and explain the steps for your country.

      Do I have to deposit copies of my book with a national library?

      Legal deposit rules exist across the GCC, usually requiring a small number of free copies to go to the national library, and in several countries the deposit is linked to ISBN registration. The receiving body and the rules differ by country: for example, the UAE uses the National Library and Archives and Saudi Arabia uses King Fahd National Library. We tell you what applies to your book and guide you through the paperwork.

      How long does it take to publish a book?

      Our typical timeline is about 2 weeks. It depends on your manuscript and the services you choose, and longer books, heavier editing, illustrations or printed proofs can add time. We agree a schedule in writing before work begins, and we do not promise unrealistic turnaround times.

      Can you publish my book on Amazon KDP and let me keep my rights?

      Yes. The KDP account is opened in your name, so the rights and the royalties remain yours. Our part is the hands-on work: formatting, metadata, categories and price set-up.

      Do you publish Arabic books?

      Yes, in Arabic, English or both, with native Arabic writers and editors in-house and right-to-left formatting for print and eBook. See the Arabic and English section above or visit the Arabic site.

      Can I publish with a Gulf-based publisher if I live outside the Gulf?

      Yes. Authors in Jordan, India, Egypt, Nigeria, Bangladesh, Iraq and other countries publish with us entirely online. We handle editing, design, formatting and platform set-up, explain which ISBN and registration route suits your country, and plan your launch so your book can reach readers in the Gulf as well as at home.

      Is the consultation really free?

      Yes. The first half-hour call costs nothing and carries no obligation. We go through your manuscript and goals, send you an itemised quote, and you pay only if you choose to proceed. A free cover concept and a short sample edit of your opening pages are included, so you can judge our work first.

      What is your refund policy?

      Our packages are covered by a 100% refund policy. The full terms are on the refund policy page.

      No. Gulf Books Publisher operates only from gulfbookspublisher.com and is not affiliated with other companies that have similar names. See our About Us page for Office Address, Contact No & Official Email Address.
